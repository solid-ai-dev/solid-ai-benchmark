# 10. Quality requirements

The quality requirements, one section per ISO/IEC 25010:2023
characteristic, are kept with the functional requirements in
[requirements.md](../requirements.md), so every requirement lives in one
place under one ID scheme. Chapter 1 ranks the five quality goals they make
measurable.

The measurements behind them, the hidden suite's validation, the trial
bounds and every run of the judge's control fixture, are in
[validation.md](../validation.md).
